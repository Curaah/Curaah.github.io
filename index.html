CareConnect — 5 Core Innovations

Five features that do not exist
anywhere in India today.

Every feature solves a real coordination failure. Together, they create a network effect that gets stronger the more organizations and volunteers join.

01 — FLAGSHIP
Privacy-First Relay System
Volunteers and organizations coordinate without ever sharing personal contact details. All messages route through the Curaah relay — like Ola's masked calling, but for community health. Volunteer consent is a dial, not a switch: Level 0 (relay only) → Level 1 (first name + city) → Level 2 (full contact, 3-tap confirm, always revocable). An NGO running a camp in Amritsar will never see a volunteer's phone number unless that volunteer explicitly consents, per interaction, every time.
02
Blood Emergency 3-Track Dispatch
One patient request fires three parallel tracks simultaneously: blood banks within 100km, NGOs with blood coordination experience, and opted-in volunteers with matching blood group. First confirmed response wins. Patient sees live status. All communication through relay — no contact shared until patient approves.
03
GPS-Smart Notifications
Events are not broadcast to everyone. A diabetes camp notifies only diabetics in the relevant area. A women's health drive notifies only women. The feed is open to all in-app — but every alert is surgically targeted. Right person, right time, zero noise.
04
Cross-Org Coordination
NGOs discover each other, share mobile vans, co-manage events, and respond to cross-city resource needs — all through the relay. An NGO in Chandigarh can share their health van with an NGO in Ropar for the weekend. Logged. Impact credited to both.
05
WhatsApp AI Poster Sharing
Every posted event auto-generates an AI-designed image poster and pre-written message in English, Hindi, and Punjabi. One tap to share on WhatsApp. Extends platform reach to people who have never installed the app. The community grows itself.
+
Resource Coordination Board
Post a need or offer — hospital beds, ECG machines, wheelchairs, surplus medicines, shared mobile vans. AI matches donors with recipients. Tax documentation auto-generated. Impact logged. Donor gets an impact receipt. Completely new for India.

Privacy Architecture

Privacy is not a feature.
It is the foundation.

Every interaction, every message, every piece of data on Curaah is governed by rules that cannot be overridden — not by orgs, not by admins, not by us.

What Never Happens
  • ✗Volunteer's phone number or address shared with NGO without 3-tap explicit consent
  • ✗Patient health data shared with any third party — ever. Under any circumstances.
  • ✗Patient conditions visible to NGOs, volunteers, or healthcare professionals
  • ✗Patient-to-patient messaging, posting, or comment sections — ever
  • ✗SOS dispatched to strangers — family contacts you chose, only
  • ✗Health data pooled for advertising, profiling, or third-party sale
What Always Happens
  • ✓Consent Dial — Level 0: relay only. Level 1: name + city. Level 2: full contact (3-tap, revocable anytime)
  • ✓Relay Messaging — all messages routed through platform. Phone numbers never stored in the messages table. Ever.
  • ✓Granular volunteer consent — blood donation, emergency response, area — all separate decisions. Per interaction.
  • ✓DPDP 2023 Compliant — right to erasure honored within 72 hours. Account deletion removes all data.
  • ✓Patient AI is private — Health Twin uses your data only to serve you. Never pooled.
Emergency System

SOS for family. Requests for community.
Two separate, safe systems.

An SOS is a private alarm for your loved ones. An emergency request is a structured call to the community. Both are privacy-protected. Neither dispatches strangers without your knowledge.

SOS Button
Family Only. Zero Strangers. Always.
Instantly alerts your pre-added emergency contacts by WhatsApp and SMS with your live GPS location. Updates every 2 minutes until you press "I'm Safe." No volunteers involved. No dispatch. Just your family, right away.
  • → 2-tap confirm prevents accidental triggers
  • → Emergency contacts must consent before being added
  • → Event logged with timestamp for medical records
